bauma CHINA, Asia's leading trade fair for construction machinery, building material machines, mining machines and construction vehicles, returns to Shanghai this November. Dingli is among the major Chinese manufacturers taking part.

## Key details

- **Dates:** 24-27 November 2026
- **Venue:** Shanghai New International Expo Centre (SNIEC), Shanghai, China
- **Dingli booth:** To be announced
- **Official site:** [bauma-china.com](https://www.bauma-china.com/)

New for 2026, the show runs across two venues. Alongside the main exhibition at SNIEC, the Shanghai World Expo Exhibition & Convention Center opens a day earlier, 23-26 November, with a focus on future technologies and industrial ecosystems, while SNIEC hosts the main machinery exhibition.

## What Dingli builds

Dingli is one of the world's largest manufacturers of aerial work platforms, with one of the broadest ranges in the industry:

- **Boom lifts** - telescopic and articulated, up to the [BT44RT](/equipment/bt44rt) at 44.44 m working height
- **Scissor lifts** - electric slab scissors for indoor work and rough terrain models for site work
- **Vertical masts and stock pickers** - compact access for warehouses and facilities
- **AC+ all-electric range** - oil-free machines for zero-emission and indoor sites ([see the AC+ Series](/ac-plus-series))

The machines on show at the stand will be confirmed closer to the event. We'll update this post with the booth number and highlights once they're announced.
